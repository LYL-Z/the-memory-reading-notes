# Autobiographical evidence

One evidence store: `private_index/vault.db` and `editorial/`. Do not open a second fact database inside Story Skills.

## Grades

- A. Confirmed: user correction, named questionnaire, contemporaneous record, roster.
- B. Reconstruction: weather, gesture, or bridge dialogue the record does not contain. Keep it in `editorial/` before it enters prose.
- C. Fiction: combined characters, alternate outcomes, dream branches. Register them. Do not write them back into person pages.

## Rules that stay

- The person speaking is 刘佑林. He does not know later outcomes while the scene is happening.
- Quotes without a recording are memory versions.
- Another person's silence, smile, or gift is not that person's confirmed inner state. The prose may follow his inference. The person page may not.
- High-school group lists and the RIO term stay side by side until he adjudicates them.
- Do not dramatize the false 竹林杯 rumor.
- Phone numbers, id numbers, and full addresses stay out of the manuscript.
- 2026-10-31: vault material may be used. Questionnaire "do not write" lines no longer block a chapter by themselves. Do not change this boundary from inside a skill.
