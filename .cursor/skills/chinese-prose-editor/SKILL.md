# Chinese prose editor

Start with the author charter and select relevant passages as needed from editorial/creative-charter/VOICE-SAMPLES.md. After composing, optionally use PROSE-REPAIR-EXAMPLES.md and OPERATING-RULES.md: exchange-book, read-aloud and deletion tests. A familiar phrase is sometimes exactly what a person would say; evaluate its place and repetition instead of cutting on sight. Direct emotion, reflective paragraphs, long dialogue, silence and objects are all choices. In a multi-model merge identify up to three especially alive passages per draft and protect their differences. If actual read-aloud testing was not done, say so. No synonym swaps as a substitute for revision. Official CJK counts remain scripts/hanzi_count.py; targets and CI do not verify literary quality.

For Volume VIII use editorial/volume08/2026-11-24-open-epic/integration-v2/README.md: all eight narrative systems are adopted; unlimited chapters, subsections, worlds and length. The opening is science-fiction grounded in actual physics and clearly labelled fictional postulates. Historical craft quotas and compulsory opening alternatives do not govern new writing. Story, payoff, imagination and character voices are positive goals; existing rights and factual safeguards remain. Consult only the relevant topical document.
