# Cinematic scene engine

Read AGENTS. Use editorial/creative-charter/templates/material-card.md and chapter-brief.md with existing work/scene_cards/ as appropriate. Find the gap between what people want, know, say and do; a quiet afternoon is also a possible scene. When useful, try different openings or information orders; otherwise continue the scene directly. A chapter may contain multiple subsections or span years. Film supplies ways of structuring time, not copied plots, images or a shot list. L1 ordinary dialogue, weather and gestures may be reconstructed when useful; do not present new major real events as fact. A successful choice need not carry symmetrical punishment. Write prose, not a planning report.

For Volume VIII use editorial/volume08/2026-11-24-open-epic/integration-v2/README.md: all eight narrative systems are adopted; unlimited chapters, subsections, worlds and length. The opening is science-fiction grounded in actual physics and clearly labelled fictional postulates. Historical craft quotas and compulsory opening alternatives do not govern new writing. Story, payoff, imagination and character voices are positive goals; existing rights and factual safeguards remain. Consult only the relevant topical document.
