# Cinematic scene engine

A scene card lives in `work/scene_cards/` and then enters the chapter. It is not a screenplay and not a Story Skills scene file of its own.

## Card

Who is present. What he wants. What stops him. What he does. What changes. What it costs. Real time, and what the reader newly knows.

## From film, only the question

Nolan is for a rearranged fact that changes a feeling. A look back across years is for an object that returns. Daily friction is for status and self-respect inside an ordinary hour. Do not copy a ship, a pose, a workplace romance, or a line.

Write the novel sentence, not "镜头", "切到", or "特写", unless a character is actually talking about a movie.

If the record is only "the relationship cooled", find the note, the seat, and the people in the room before inventing rain, a phone call, or a kiss.
