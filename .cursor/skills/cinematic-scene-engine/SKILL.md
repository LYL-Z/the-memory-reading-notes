# Cinematic scene engine

Read AGENTS. Use editorial/creative-charter/templates/material-card.md and chapter-brief.md with existing work/scene_cards/ as appropriate. Find the gap between what people want, know, say and do; a quiet afternoon is also a possible scene. Think of three openings with different entrances or information orders, then choose the strongest artistic risk consistent with the operating rules. Film supplies ways of structuring time, not copied plots, images or a shot list. L1 ordinary dialogue, weather and gestures may be reconstructed when useful; do not present new major real events as fact. A successful choice need not carry symmetrical punishment. Write prose, not a planning report.
