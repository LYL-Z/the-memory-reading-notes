# Literary romance craft

Write the cost of wanting to move closer and not moving. Do not use the words 禁忌 or 沦陷 as a substitute for a scene.

## Age

- Under 18: attraction, misunderstanding, shame, restraint, and consequence. No explicit sexual description.
- From age 18: the 2026-10-31 authorization allows the recorded material, including adult intimacy that is not a graphic sexual act.
- Graphic sexual acts stay out of scenes where a person is under 21.

## People

- 赵问竹: his confirmation on 2026-09-16 is not her transcript. Her silence is not an answer. Do not merge her with 董佳好.
- 董佳好: classmate and long-term chat friend. Chat frequency is not a relationship name.
- Do not assign a sexual act, illness, or crime to an identifiable real person because a chapter needs heat.

One inference per scene. The younger self may be wrong. The older self may think something else. Do not stack both as if they were her mind.
