# Literary romance craft

Read the adult gaze section of AGENTS and the maturity and rights section of editorial/creative-charter/OPERATING-RULES.md. A character may directly express love, desire, disappointment, joy or embarrassment. Ordinary shared life, humor and a fulfilled relationship can be as valuable as restraint or loss. Age 18 and above supports mature themes and non-graphic intimacy; the former 21-year threshold for mature themes is obsolete. Minors are not sexualized; do not invent sexual histories or eroticize identifiable real people. Use genuinely independent fictional adults for invented intimate private lives. Consent is distinct from silence, intoxication or physical reaction. Real-life relationship evidence and alternate-world choices never establish each other; public release rights remain separate. Do not prescribe a fixed number of inferences, shame beats or moral endings.
