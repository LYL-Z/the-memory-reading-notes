## 科学

Sorci, Gabriel, et al. “Quantum Signatures of Proper Time in Optical Ion Clocks.” arXiv, 11 Sept. 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09573. NIST publication page dated 20 Apr. 2026, https://www.nist.gov/publications/quantum-signatures-proper-time-optical-ion-clocks. 读了摘要与文中可见的 `²⁷Al⁺`、`ε_m`、`r` 与可见度估算，没有重推全文哈密顿量。文中效应是预言的可观测性，不是已完成的穿越实验。
