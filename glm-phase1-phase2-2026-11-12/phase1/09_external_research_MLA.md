## 二、逐条引文与验证级别（r2 实抓）

### SRC-01｜Shen, "Unreliability" — `FULL_TEXT`（51,754 字符）
> "In its narratological sense, unreliability is a feature of narratorial discourse. If a narrator misreports, -interprets or -evaluates, or if she/he underreports, -interprets or -evaluates, this narrator is unreliable or untrustworthy."

> "In Booth's view, a narrator is '**reliable** when he speaks for or acts in accordance with the norms of the work (which is to say the implied author's norms), **unreliable** when he does not' (Booth [1961] 1983: 158–59)."

> "Phelan points out that narrators 'perform three main roles—reporting, interpreting, and evaluating' (2005: 50). In light of these three roles, Phelan classifies unreliability by focusing on three axes: the axis of facts; the axis of values or ethics; and the axis of knowledge and perception."

> "Phelan identifies six types of unreliability which fall into two larger categories: (1) misreporting, misinterpreting (misreading) and misevaluating (misregarding); (2) underreporting, underinterpreting (underreading), and underevaluating (underregarding). The contrast between the 'mis-' category and the 'under-' category is basically a contrast between **being wrong and being insufficient**."

**用途**：把「不可靠」拆成事实／价值／知识三轴与「错」／「不足」两类，避免把全书压成一句「叙述者不可靠」。**关键**：本书叙述者主要是 **under-（信息不足）＋有限知觉**，不是 mis-（蓄意错报）。

### SRC-02｜Niederhoff, "Focalization" — `FULL_TEXT`（35,566 字符）
> "Focalization, a term coined by Genette ([1972]), may be defined as a selection or restriction of narrative information in relation to the experience and knowledge of the narrator, the characters or other, more hypothetical entities in the storyworld."

> "Genette believes that such cavalier treatments of the subject 'suffer from a regrettable confusion [...] between the question *who is the character whose point of view orients the narrative perspective?* and the very different question *who is the narrator*—or, more simply, the question *who sees?* and the question *who speaks?*' (1980: 186)."

> "In the second term [internal focalization], *Narrator = Character* (the narrator says only what a given character knows) [...] In the third term [external focalization], *Narrator < Character* (the narrator says less than the character knows)."

**用途**：区分「谁看／谁讲」后，才能审 V1-001（成年叙述者在场但只给当时的信息）、V1-015（回望判断与可见动作是否分开）、V2-019（误认是否严格停在「我看见的背影」层）。

### SRC-03｜Schwalm, "Autobiography" — `FULL_TEXT`（56,225 字符）
> "While autobiography on the one hand claims to be non-fictional (factual) in that it proposes to tell the story of a 'real' person, it is **inevitably constructive, or imaginative, in nature** and as a form of textual 'self-fashioning' ultimately resists a clear distinction from its fictional relatives (autofiction, autobiographical novel), leaving the generic borderlines blurred."

> "Comprehensive and continuous retrospection, based on memory, makes up its governing structural and semantic principle."

> "At the heart of its narrative logic lies the duality of the autobiographical person, divided into '**narrating I**' and '**narrated I**', marking the distance between the experiencing and the narrating subject."

> "The narrative present, then, can only ever be a temporary point of view, affording an 'interim balance' (de Bruyn 1994) at best, leaving the final vantage point an autobiographical illusion."

**用途**：V2-001（童年一人分饰三役）与 V2-018（评语里的「我」与自传的「我」）的体裁学坐标；「interim balance」可直接解释 V2 卷末「写下一行」为何不是升华而是诚实。

## 五、使用边界（写给下一阶段）

1. 理论条目作**审稿问题单**使用，不写进报告正文当装饰；不把「诺兰感」「石黑一雄式」当成结论。

3. 中文作家只提供技法参照，**不得**暗示本书模仿其文风；比较停在结构层（物件链、固定空间、信息限制）。
4. **只迁移技法，不复制任何在世创作者的可辨识语气、固定句式或原段落**；不把影视台词、收藏名言当作 2023 年真实对话。

6. 外部联网材料只能补充时代／专业背景，**不能替代主角亲历的证据或凭空补回自己忘记的场景**。
